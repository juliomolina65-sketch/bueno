HOW OUR MESSAGING WORKS

Bueno sends one kind of text message, to one kind of person: someone who just called a local business and didn't get an answer.

When a message is sent

1

You call a local business — a repair shop, a barbershop, a restaurant — because you want to reach them.

2

Nobody picks up. The business has asked us to catch those calls instead of sending you to voicemail.

3

We text you back once, from that business, with a link so you can book a time, ask for a callback, request a quote, or get directions.

Your own call is the consent. You reached out to the business first, and our reply goes only to the number you called from. If you never call, you never hear from us.

What the message looks like

Example
Sorry we missed you at Marino & Sons Auto! Tap here and we'll take care of you right away: buenoanswers.com/b/marinos-auto

The details

FREQUENCYOne message per unanswered call. No recurring campaigns, no follow-up blasts.
STOPReply STOP to any message and we stop texting you. It takes effect immediately.
HELPReply HELP and we'll reply with how to reach the business and how to reach us.
RATESMessage and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

What we never do

  • Send marketing, promotions, or sales messages
  • Text anyone who has not called the business first
  • Buy, rent, or use purchased phone number lists
  • Sell or share your number with anyone for their own marketing

Questions or requests

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email hello@buenoanswers.com and we'll respond within 30 days. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
