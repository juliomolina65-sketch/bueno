Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Your number stays exactly as it is, everywhere. One forwarding setting turns the rescue on — dialed from your phone in under a minute, and we do it with you.

What happens when I DO answer?

Nothing. The call connects like normal. The rescue only wakes up when you can't get there.

Is texting customers legal?

Yes — this texts only people who just called you, one time, as a response. That's the compliant kind. We never blast, never spam, never text strangers.

What if it doesn't rescue anything?

Your monthly report shows every rescued call. If it's not earning its keep, cancel — month to month, no contract, no hard feelings.
